# BiteWise product north star

- Position BiteWise as one app combining calorie/macro tracking with smartwatch fitness, activity, sleep, and recovery data—not two adjacent dashboards.
- Make Diary the daily product spine: capture/log → confirm → understand → act → repeat. Foods captures; Goals defines targets; Wellness interprets connected health signals; Statistics explains trends; Connections exclusively owns provider setup.
- Near-term priority is a trustworthy, cohesive foundation before advanced personalization.
- Future AI should explain evidence-backed relationships between nutrition and fitness/recovery (for example meal timing vs sleep, calorie deficit vs next-day HRV, or training-day carbohydrate intake vs recovery), with minimum data thresholds, confidence/caveats, provenance, non-causal language, and no invented or automatic target changes.
- Keep Thomas proactively updated at major delivery checkpoints without flooding him with routine worker notifications.
